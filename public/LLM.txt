# Business Description
ChopTym is a reliable delivery and errand service company based in Limbe, Cameroon.
We specialize in same-day deliveries, personal errands, document handling, and food delivery.
Our service is built on trust, reliability, and professional communication via WhatsApp.

# Services Offered
1. Same-Day Delivery Service - Package pickups and deliveries within Limbe
2. Personal Errand Service - Bill payments, queue waiting, prescription pickups
3. Document Handling - Contract delivery, official document submission
4. Food Delivery - Restaurant and home cook food delivery in Limbe
5. Market Shopping - Grocery and market item pickup and delivery
6. Inter-City Coordination - Deliveries from other Cameroonian towns to Limbe
